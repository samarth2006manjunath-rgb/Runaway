Concept · undergrad coursework app

Runway

Every assignment, syllabus date, class and course email in one place — ordered by when you have to start, not when it's due.

The problem

Work is scattered across the LMS, five syllabi and an inbox, so nothing is visible until it's a few hours from due.

The move

Each item carries an estimate and a start by date. The app schedules backwards from the deadline and tells you what today is for.

Color as signal

Courses are lettermarks, not colors. The one accent is reserved for anything you should start now.
